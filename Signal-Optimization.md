# Signal Optimization Framework

Classification quality is heavily dependent on keyword quality.

The framework followed a six-stage optimization process.

## 1. Keyword Identification

Keywords were collected from historical communications and quality reviews.

## 2. Frequency Analysis

Keywords were ranked by occurrence frequency.

## 3. Heatmap Analysis

Visual analysis was used to identify concentration patterns.

## 4. Weight Distribution Analysis

High-frequency keywords initially dominated category identification.

## 5. Context Expansion

Synonyms and contextual variations were introduced to improve coverage.

## 6. Revalidation

Categories were re-audited to measure improvement in confidence and consistency.

---

## Outcome

The final framework contained:

- 45 Categories
- 692 Keywords
- Balanced keyword distribution
- Improved category coverage
