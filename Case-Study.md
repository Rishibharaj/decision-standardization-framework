# Case Study: Decision Standardization in Consumer Communications

## Business Context

Consumer-facing operations reviewed more than 89,000 customer communications across multiple channels, including letters, emails, and SMS messages.

The objective was to ensure that similar customer concerns resulted in consistent operational outcomes regardless of communication format or reviewer interpretation.

---

## Challenge

Variations in individual interpretation created inconsistencies in decision-making, increased audit complexity, and introduced operational risk.

The challenge was not processing communications.

The challenge was ensuring that the same decision would be reached consistently across reviewers and communication channels.

---

## Framework Applied

The Decision Standardization Framework combined:

- Signal Interpretation
- Decision Logic
- Precedence Rules
- Governance Controls
- Statistical Validation

to transform unstructured communications into consistent business outcomes.

---
## Example

### Consumer Communication

"I do not want phone calls and do not contact me again."

### Signals Identified

- Call Suppression
- Full Contact Restriction

### Precedence Rule

Where multiple signals are identified, precedence rules determine which outcome best represents the consumer's request.

In this scenario, **Full Contact Restriction** supersedes **Call Suppression** because it represents the broader consumer instruction.

### Outcome

Full Contact Restriction

---
## Scope

- 89,000+ communications analyzed
- 20,000 audited samples
- 45 standardized decision scenarios
- Multiple communication channels
- Validation confidence levels ranging from 95% to 99%

---

## Outcomes

- Reduced interpretation variability
- Standardized decision-making
- Improved auditability and governance
- Increased consistency across communication channels
- Established a foundation for automation readiness

---

## Core Insight

Before automation becomes possible, decisions must become predictable.

Consistency created the opportunity.

Technology simply enabled it.
