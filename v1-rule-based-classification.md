# V1 – Rule-Based Classification Framework

## Objective

Create a repeatable and auditable method for classifying consumer communications.

## Framework Design

Keywords
↓
Categories
↓
Precedence Rules
↓
Outcome

### Layer 1 – Keyword Mapping

Keywords are mapped to categories.

### Layer 2 – Category Identification

Detected keywords trigger categories.

### Layer 3 – Precedence Rules

When multiple categories are identified, priority rules determine the final outcome.

### Layer 4 – Outcome Generation

The highest-precedence category becomes the final result.
