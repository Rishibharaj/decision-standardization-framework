# Decision Intelligence Engine

The Decision Intelligence Engine converts correspondence insights into consistent business decisions by applying policy rules, precedence logic, and operational governance.

---

## Step 1: Correspondence Signals

The Correspondence Intelligence Layer identifies relevant consumer signals and categories from unstructured communications.

Examples:

- Payment dispute
- Contact restriction
- Complaint escalation
- Documentation request
- Account inquiry

↓

## Step 2: Decision Evaluation

Detected categories are evaluated against business rules and operating policies.

This layer determines:

- Priority
- Risk
- Escalation requirements
- Action eligibility

↓

## Step 3: Conflict Resolution

Where multiple categories are detected, precedence rules determine which outcome best represents the consumer's request.

Example:

Consumer Input:

"I do not want phone calls and do not contact me again."

Detected Categories:

- Call Suppression
- Full Contact Restriction

Decision Rule:

Full Contact Restriction supersedes Call Suppression because it represents the broader instruction.

↓

## Step 4: Decision Outcome

A single standardized business outcome is generated.

Examples:

- Escalate
- Route
- Restrict Contact
- Request Documentation
- Continue Standard Handling

↓

## Step 5: Governance Review

Decision outputs are validated through sampling, confidence assessment, and quality audits.

↓

## Step 6: Operational Action

Validated decisions support:

- Case routing
- Agent guidance
- Compliance controls
- Operational reporting
- Workflow automation
