# Governance Principles

The framework was intentionally designed to remain transparent, auditable, and explainable.

## Explainability

Every classification outcome can be traced back to underlying rules.

## Auditability

Decision paths can be reviewed and validated.

## Consistency

Identical inputs produce identical outputs.

## Maintainability

Rules can be updated without redesigning the framework.

## Transparency

Users can understand how outcomes were generated.
